Astrophysics

Institut d’Astrophysique de Paris · Research internship

Measuring galaxy clusters

Supervised by Dr. Gary Mamon

How do star formation and morphology vary across a cluster’s galaxy population? Before interpreting those radial trends, we need to understand which galaxies the cluster contributes to the observations.

I developed a point-process refinement model for candidates in existing cluster catalogues, treating the primary galaxy distribution and its uncertain environment together.
